## Purpose and scope

Algebraic reformulation: ACR in `S` for rate constants `k` means the positive real part of the
steady-state variety `V_k = {x > 0 : f_k(x) = 0}` lies in a hyperplane `x_S = c`. A sufficient
algebraic condition: `x_S − c` (or a power) lies in the ideal generated by the steady-state
polynomials, saturated by `∏ x_i` — checkable from a Gröbner-type oracle certificate (cofactors
`g_j` with `x^a (x_S − c) = Σ g_j f_j`). This is exactly a G-3 style certificate and is a ready
Lean target once the kernel exists. Necessity is a real-algebraic question (the ideal criterion can
fail when ACR holds only on the positive real part).

Candidate literature (**not supplied, citations unverified**): Karp et al. 2012 (complex-linear
invariants), Puente et al. on ACR algebra and geometry, Meshkat–Shiu–Torres on ACR and
dynamically equivalent networks.

## First general result: independent unions (2026-11-27, `LeanTheory/Theory/CRN/Union.lean`)

`N₀ ∪ N₁` (same species, no common complex; `ReactionNetwork.union`) has field `f₀ + f₁` and
stoichiometric subspace `S₀ ⊔ S₁`. If `S₀ ⊓ S₁ = ⊥`:

- at a positive steady state `f₀(x) = −f₁(x) ∈ S₀ ∩ S₁ = 0`, so steady states of the union are
  steady states of both parts (`isPosSteadyState_left_of_union`, `..._right_of_union`);
- **ACR is inherited** (`hasACR_union`), whatever the coupling through shared species;
- **deficiencies add** (`deficiency_union`: complexes and linkage classes add, ranks add);
- with Shinar–Feinberg for `N₀`: ACR at **arbitrary deficiency** (`hasACR_union_of_deficiency_one`).

Example (`Sanity/CRNUnion.lean`): `A + B → 2B`, `B → A` with `A + C → A + D`, `D → C` — the ACR
species `A` catalyzes the second module, the union has deficiency 2, and has nontrivial ACR in `A`
(`x_A = κ₁/κ₀`).

Scope: a sufficient condition. It does not cover networks whose ACR needs interaction between
independent-looking parts; next candidates: (i) unions where only `S₀ ∩ S₁` is controlled through
the field (e.g. `f₀(x) ∈ S₀ ∩ S₁` with a dimension count as in Shinar–Feinberg), (ii) the
linkage-class form of the deficiency-one theorem (Feinberg's independence condition `δ = Σ δ_L`
with the nonterminal pair in a class of deficiency `≤ 1`), (iii) complex-linear invariants (Karp et
al.).

## Linkage-class form (2026-11-28, `LeanTheory/Theory/CRN/LinkageClassACR.lean`)

For a set `P` of complexes closed under reactions: `E_P` = span of edge vectors of reactions
leaving `P` (`edgeSpanOn`), `S_P = Y(E_P)` = span of their reaction vectors (`stoichOn`,
`stoichOn_eq_span`).

- `hasACR_of_partDeficiency`: if `S_P ∩ S_{P^c} = 0`, `dim(E_P ∩ ker Y) ≤ 1`, and `P` contains two
  distinct nonterminal complexes differing only in `s`, then ACR in `s` for all positive rates.
  Proof: mask `ψ` to `P` (`maskOn`); `A_k(ψ|_P) ∈ E_P` and, by independence, `∈ ker Y` at a steady
  state; then the Shinar–Feinberg dependence and kernel lemma; the finish is factored out as
  `eq_of_cross_dependence`.
- `finrank_edgeSpanOn_inf_ker_le`: `dim(E_P ∩ ker Y) ≤ n_P − ℓ_P − s_P` (the part deficiency), with
  `ℓ_P` from a labelling with representatives; `E_P` is spanned by the spanning-forest vectors
  `e_c − e_{rep(c)}` (`finrank_edgeSpanOn_le`). Only this inequality is needed.
- `hasACR_of_deficiency_one'`: `P` = all complexes recovers Shinar–Feinberg (consistency check).
- `Sanity.uNet_hasACR_direct`: applied **directly** to the deficiency-two network
  `A + B → 2B, B → A, A + C → A + D, D → C` with `P` = the first module (`δ_P ≤ 4 − 2 − 1`).

Relation to the union theorem: mathematically equivalent (a network is the union of its part `P`
and the complement), but this form works on a given network without rebuilding it. Next for Q-1:
pairs whose classes are not stoichiometrically independent of the rest (a dimension count on the
projection of `S_P ∩ S_{P^c}`), and complex-linear invariants.
