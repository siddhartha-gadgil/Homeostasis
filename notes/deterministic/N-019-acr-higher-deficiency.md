## Purpose and scope

Algebraic reformulation: ACR in `S` for rate constants `k` means the positive real part of the
steady-state variety `V_k = {x > 0 : f_k(x) = 0}` lies in a hyperplane `x_S = c`. A sufficient
algebraic condition: `x_S − c` (or a power) lies in the ideal generated by the steady-state
polynomials, saturated by `∏ x_i` — checkable from a Gröbner-type oracle certificate (cofactors
`g_j` with `x^a (x_S − c) = Σ g_j f_j`). This is exactly a G-3 style certificate and is a ready
Lean target once the kernel exists. Necessity is a real-algebraic question (the ideal criterion can
fail when ACR holds only on the positive real part).

Candidate literature (**not supplied, citations unverified**): Karp et al. 2012 (complex-linear
invariants), Puente et al. on ACR algebra and geometry, Meshkat–Shiu–Torres on ACR and
dynamically equivalent networks.
