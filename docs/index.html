The program

Homeostasis in biochemistry comes in several mathematically distinct forms. The project studies them in deterministic mass-action models ẋ = Γ v(x) on the positive orthant and in their stochastic counterparts (the chemical master equation on copy numbers), and treats Lean as the validity gate: a result counts as established only once a named theorem compiles.

Notions of homeostasis

Kept as distinct predicates in Lean and linked only by proved implications, with examples separating them.

Absolute concentration robustness

For fixed rates, every positive steady state has the same value of a species, across all compatibility classes (Shinar–Feinberg). Vacuous without steady states and trivial with a unique one, so headline claims use HasACRNontrivial.

Perfect adaptation

The output at equilibrium does not depend on an input, over a set of inputs; stability is a separate contract (HasRPALinear adds it).

Infinitesimal homeostasis

The derivative of the output along a branch of equilibria vanishes; for networks it is equivalent to det H = 0 for the homeostasis matrix (Golubitsky–Stewart).

Stochastic homeostasis

What the chemical master equation keeps: stationary means (antithetic feedback) or nothing at all (extinction in ACR networks).

A first example: EnvZ–OmpR

In the osmoregulation system EnvZ–OmpR of E. coli, the level of phosphorylated OmpR barely depends on the amounts of EnvZ and OmpR. Shinar and Feinberg (Science, 2010) explained this as ACR of the mass-action model. The value is proved in Lean for all positive rate constants, from a polynomial identity found by an untrusted oracle:

/-- **ACR in OmpR-P for all positive rate constants**: every positive steady state has
`[OmpR-P] = envzACRValue k`. -/

The ingredients

Kernel

Reaction networks over arbitrary finite types; stoichiometric subspace, linkage classes, deficiency; the mass-action field; ACR, infinitesimal homeostasis and perfect adaptation as separate predicates, joined only by proved implications.

Sanity checks

Every notion has a degenerate case, an instance and a counterexample. ACR is vacuous without positive steady states and trivial when the steady state is unique, so headline claims use HasACRNontrivial.

Certificates

Untrusted oracles (sympy) find cofactor identities, Lyapunov matrices and LDLᵀ factorizations; Lean checks them with ring, linear_combination and exact rational arithmetic.

Stability

Linearization with a Lyapunov certificate, the converse Lyapunov theorem, Routh–Hurwitz for 3×3 matrices, and stability relative to a compatibility class for networks with conservation laws.

Stochastic layer

The chemical master equation as a generator; finite closed state sets give a rate matrix and a uniformized chain, with absorption theorems and stationary means.

Research graph

Goals, questions and notes with stable identifiers (G-*, Q-*, N-*); a claim becomes a dependency only when a named compiled theorem matches it.

Every steady state is class-stable for IDHKP–IDH at all rates and for both EnvZ–OmpR models at unit rates. General rates need certificates for polynomial identities with tens of thousands of terms.
